Error design tracked, removed c1546 makes everything works
ProMicro 3V3 VCC
      │                │
    4.7kΩ (C25900)   4.7kΩ (C25900)   ← pull-ups
      │                │
  ProMicro SDA ────── ProMicro SCL
      │                │
    100pF (C1546)    100pF (C1546)     ← NEW filter caps
      │                │
    GND              GND
  The 100pF capacitors (C1546) on SDA and SCL are killing the I2C signals.

 The rise time formula: 2.2 × R × C = 2.2 × 4700 × 100pF ≈ 1034ns
  Even at 100kHz standard mode it's right at the limit. The SDA/SCL signals can't rise fast enough — the OLED (and IQS5xx) see malformed
  I2C and don't respond.
